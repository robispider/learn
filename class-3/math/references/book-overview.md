## How the book teaches (patterns to keep)

### Characters (p.5)
- **রাফি (Rafi)** — boy; often asks "কীভাবে…?", proposes a first idea, sometimes a wrong one.
- **তুলি (Tuli)** — girl; refines the idea, states the method or rule.
- **টিয়া (Tia)** — green parakeet; gives the formal definition, asks "think of another way" / "explain to a friend".

Dialogue in coloured speech boxes: Rafi = yellow, Tuli = pink, Tia = light blue. Rules/definitions in a **lavender rounded box**.

### Four activity symbols (p.5) — the learning flow of every topic
| Symbol | Name | Meaning in the book | Our app mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| ❓ speech-bubble icon | **মূল প্রশ্ন** (main question) | "Let's all solve together" — a real-life hook | **Explore** — the story + interactive toy |
| ▲ pink triangle | **কাজ** (activity) | Solve with classmates & teacher | **Try together** — guided, step-by-step with hints |
| ■ teal square | **অনুশীলন** (practice) | Think logically, ask a friend/teacher if needed | **Practice** — varied, auto-generated items with feedback |
| ● blue circle | **নিজে করি** (do it myself) | Solve alone (end of chapter) | **Challenge / Quest** — mixed review, mastery check |

### Recurring visual language
- Place-value colours (keep app-wide): **১০০০০ yellow-green · ১০০০ peach · ১০০ teal · ১০ orange · ১ pink**.
- Headings ক / খ / গ / ঘ for MCQ options; "খালিঘর পূরণ করি" (fill the box), "দাগ টেনে মিল করি" (match with lines), "সঠিক উত্তরে টিক (✓) দিই".
- **4-step frame strips** with arrows and a coloured callout under the active column (addition, subtraction, multiplication).
- "Shield" shaped vertical-algorithm frames with a small blue arrow showing which digits multiply.
- **গাণিতিক বাক্য** (number sentence) box before solving; answer with units.
- Real-life Bangladeshi contexts: taka & paisa, mango orchards, ilish, hali/dozen/dista, Eid, Baishakh, flood relief, train stations, book fair, shishu park, village maps.
- Every chapter ends with "make a story for this number sentence" → creativity & language.

### Pedagogical sequence used throughout
Real-life question → concrete objects (blocks, biscuits, money) → picture/diagram (arrays, tapes, place-value chart) → symbols & algorithm → practice → word problems → create your own problem. This is the **Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract (CPA)** approach — our app should follow it literally.
