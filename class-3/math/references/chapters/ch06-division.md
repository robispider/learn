# অধ্যায় ৬ — ভাগ (Division)

- **Book pages:** 63–77 · **PDF pages:** 69–83
- **Range:** facts within tables to 10; tens ÷ 1-digit (80 ÷ 4); 2-digit ÷ 1-digit by splitting (84 ÷ 4); remainders. (No long-division algorithm with 3-digit dividends yet, except an MCQ glimpse of the `)` layout and 125 ÷ 5.)

## Vocabulary
ভাগ, ÷ (ভাগ চিহ্ন), ভাজ্য, ভাজক, ভাগফল, ভাগশেষ / অবশিষ্ট, সমানভাবে (equally), প্রত্যেকে কয়টি করে পাবে? (how many each), কতজনকে দেওয়া যাবে? (how many people), বান্ডেল.

## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Plate Dealer (sharing)** — drag or "deal" biscuits round-robin to N plates; a "deal one round" button; leftovers glow; equation chips fill live. Toggle 0 biscuits (Tia's joke question).
2. **Packing Machine (grouping)** — conveyor belt packs items into bags of size k; count bags; leftovers fall into a "ভাগশেষ basket". Same numbers as Plate Dealer → compare both meanings side by side.
3. **Rafi vs Tuli error** — show 19 ÷ 3 = 5 r 4; child taps "Can we still share?" and fixes it → teaches ভাগশেষ < ভাজক.
4. **Bundle Splitter** — 84 ÷ 4: bundles of ten go to 4 friends first, then loose ones.
5. **Ribbon Cutter** — scissors cut 4 cm pieces from a 30 cm ribbon on a ruler.
6. **Times-table reverse** — "Which number × 7 = 42?" lights in the multiplication chart.
7. **Array → equation** — shapes arranged in rows; child types `ভাজ্য = ভাজক × ভাগফল + ভাগশেষ`.
