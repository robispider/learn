## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Magnifier Ruler** — drag objects (pencil, eraser, leaf) onto a ruler; zoom lens shows mm ticks; estimate first, then measure; score closeness.
2. **Unit Ladder slide** — a number slides down the ladder km → m → cm → mm (×1000, ×100, ×10) with zeros appearing.
3. **Nipa's Map trip** — tap a route; distances add up; convert to metres; "who walks farther?".
4. **Balance It!** — drag standard weights onto the balance to match a sack of 650 g; arm tilts realistically; challenge: fewest weights.
5. **Clay conservation** — squish a clay ball into a snake; scale reading stays the same (surprise moment).
6. **Clock Workshop** — drag hands (gears linked: minute drives hour); show digital twin; phrases সাড়ে/সোয়া/পৌনে; day/night sky changes behind with সকাল/দুপুর/বিকাল/রাত.
7. **Time Travel** — "+30 min" arrow animates the hands forward; elapsed-time puzzles; Sami's visit timeline.
8. **My Routine** — build a daily routine with clock stickers (personal, printable).
