## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Array Garden** — plant flowers in rows × columns by dragging a corner; product counts up; "rotate" button turns 3×5 into 5×3 (commutative), "scissors" splits an array into two (distributive).
2. **Table Builder (L-frame)** — slide the frame across a 13-wide dot grid; each slide adds a row to 13's table; colour 10-part and 3-part.
3. **Flower Power** — spinning centre number; tap petals to multiply; outer petals bloom when correct (timed mode for fluency).
4. **Multiplication Chart Explorer** — tap a cell → array appears; find patterns (diagonals, even/odd, ×5 ends in 0/5); fill 11–15 rows.
5. **Area Model Builder** — drag splitters to break 21×13 into 4 coloured rectangles; numbers show; sum them; then show the same in the vertical method side by side (link visual to algorithm).
6. **Shop multiplier** — buy n items at price p with tape diagram; receipts.
7. **Speed run** — a car moves 45 km per hour tick on a map; predict distance after 8 hours.
8. **Fact Fluency mini-game** (daily 1-min) — like "times-table rally" with streaks; adaptive to weak facts.
