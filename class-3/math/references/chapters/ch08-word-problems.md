## Key idea the book teaches
Read → decide **which operation first** → write one number sentence → solve step by step (ধাপ ১, ধাপ ২) → write the answer with unit (টাকা, টি, কেজি). Rafi says the plan, Tuli writes the sentence.

## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Story Machine (bar models)** — the story appears in pieces; each sentence animates into a bar model block (equal bars for ×, cut bars for ÷, a bite out for −). Child drags operation tiles to build the number sentence, then solves.
2. **"Which first?" sorter** — two operation cards; drag into order; see what happens if wrong (silly outcome animation, e.g., change becomes negative).
3. **Market Day** — shop scene: buy items, pay with notes, get change; mixed operations naturally arise (uses ch.10 money).
4. **Relief Camp / Eid sharing** — collect from many, share among some (× then ÷) with sacks of rice graphic.
5. **Write-your-own-story** — given `55 × 30 ÷ 50`, pick objects & people from picture cards; app fills a template; child edits words.
6. **Number Wheel puzzle** — spin & fill sectors.
