# অধ্যায় ৭ — গুণ ও ভাগের সম্পর্ক (Relation of × and ÷)

- **Book pages:** 78–83 · **PDF pages:** 84–89

## Sections in the book

| # | Topic | Book p. | What the book does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | Inverse operations | 78 | Rafi: × is repeated addition, ÷ is repeated subtraction. Table: 7 × 4 = 28 because 7+7+7+7; 28 ÷ 4 = 7 because 28−4−4…=0 (7 times); 28 ÷ 7 = 4. Fact-family trees: 6 × 3 = 18 → 18 ÷ 3 = 6, 18 ÷ 6 = 3. "Use the relation to check answers." |
| 7.2 | Fractions of a quantity as ÷ then × | 78–79 | Tahsin 40 pencils, 5 equal parts, gives Ratul 2 parts → 40 ÷ 5 = 8, 8 × 2 = 16. |
| 7.3 | Mixed ÷ and × order | 79–80 | 12 ÷ 2 × 6: "when × and ÷ sit side by side, work left to right" = 6 × 6 = 36. Drills 15 ÷ 3 × 8 etc. Two-step problems: 6 baskets × 21 mangoes ÷ 14 people = 126 ÷ 14 = 9. 4 packets × 30 chocolates ÷ 15 friends. |
| 7.4 | Unit rate (একক মূল্য) | 81 | 12 pens cost 96 ৳ → 1 pen = 96 ÷ 12 = 8 (check 12 × 8 = 96, red circles) → 32 pens = 8 × 32 = 256. 13 bananas 117 ৳ → 24 bananas? (ধাপ ১, ধাপ ২ boxes). |
| 7.5 | নিজে করি | 82–83 | MCQ: which process is correct for a story; 7+7+…(8 times); "8 ভাগের 2 ভাগ" of a day; short Qs (repeated add/subtract names). Story for 65 ÷ 13 × 4. |

## Vocabulary
বিপরীত প্রক্রিয়া, পুনঃপুন যোগ / বিয়োগ (repeated add/sub), যাচাই (check), বাম থেকে ডান (left to right), "ক ভাগের খ ভাগ" (b parts out of a — early fraction-of-quantity language), ধাপ (step).

## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Fact Family House** — a house with roof number 18 and two windows 3 and 6; four "residents" (×,×,÷,÷) knock to get in only if their sentence is true.
2. **Repeated-subtraction frog** — frog hops back by 4 from 28 to 0 on a number line; count hops = 7.
3. **Check-it stamp** — after any ÷ answer, flip the card to verify with ×; a green "যাচাই সঠিক ✓" stamp.
4. **Price Tag Lab (unit rate)** — 12 pens for 96 ৳ → shrink to 1 pen → grow to 32 pens; bar model animation.
5. **Step Builder** — drag operation tiles (× 21, ÷ 14) into a 2-step machine; output shows each step (prepares for ch.8).
