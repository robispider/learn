## Sections in the book

| # | Topic | Book p. | What the book does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Inverse operations | 37 | Tia: "We had some apples, sold 6, have 8 — how many first?" → ☐ − 6 = 8. Shows 14−6=8, 8+6=14, 14−8=6. Relationship table: বিয়োজন − বিয়োজ্য = বিয়োগফল; বিয়োগফল + বিয়োজ্য = বিয়োজন; বিয়োজন − বিয়োগফল = বিয়োজ্য. Fill-the-blank (☐ − 32 = 54). |
| 4.2 | Mixed + and − in one problem | 38 | Train with 1637 passengers, 865 got off, 730 got on → 1637 − 865 + 730. School 538 + 125 new − 93 left. |
| 4.3 | Make two stories | 39 | Diagram: "14 + 10" and "29 − 5" both → 24 (yellow circle); write a + story and a − story with the same answer. Marble story (65 + 48, then lost 17). |
| 4.4 | নিজে করি | 40–41 | MCQ on the relation; vertical drills up to 5 digits; missing-number boxes (98 + ☐ + 3607 + 2659 = 7453); **number puzzles**: star wheel where opposite pairs make a sum; diamond chains (12 ◇ 9 ◇ 3). ## Vocabulary
বিপরীত প্রক্রিয়া (inverse process), সম্পর্ক, খালিঘর পূরণ.

## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Part-Part-Whole bar** — one long bar (বিয়োজন) split into two parts. Hide any one piece → child finds it; the same picture produces all 3 facts. (Bar model used again in ch.8.)
2. **Fact triangle cards** — 14 on top, 6 and 8 at bottom; cover a corner.
3. **Train simulator** — a train at stations; passengers on/off with animations; running total.
4. **Magic star / diamond puzzles** — drag numbers so every line makes the target.
5. **Same answer, two stories** — choose a + and a − sentence with equal results, illustrated.
