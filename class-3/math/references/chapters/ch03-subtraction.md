## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Unpack the bundle** — when top digit is too small, child taps the next column's block: a ten-rod "cracks" into 10 cubes that slide right. Makes borrowing physical (fixes the classic "smaller-from-larger" error 2−5 → 5−2).
2. **Zero Tunnel** — 1000 − 26: a domino chain of unbundling through the zeros (1000 cube → 10 flats → 1 flat → 10 rods → …) with a satisfying cascade.
3. **Change Counter** — buyer pays 2000 ৳ for 1850 ৳ hilsa; count change with notes/coins.
4. **Train stop** — passengers hop off at a station; count who remains.
5. **Error spotter** — Tia made a mistake in a worked subtraction; find the wrong column.
