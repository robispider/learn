# অধ্যায় ৯ — ভগ্নাংশ (Fractions)

- **Book pages:** 91–106 · **PDF pages:** 97–112
- **Scope:** unit fractions ½, ¼, ⅓ → non-unit (3/5, 7/8) → লব/হর → fractions on a 1-metre tape / number line → comparing same-denominator fractions → equivalent fractions (multiply top & bottom; cross-multiplication check) → adding & subtracting with same denominator, incl. 1 − 3/8.

## Vocabulary
ভগ্নাংশ (fraction), অর্ধেক (half), এক চতুর্থাংশ (quarter), এক তৃতীয়াংশ (third), দুই-তৃতীয়াংশ, "ক ভাগের খ ভাগ" (b out of a parts), লব (numerator), হর (denominator), সমতুল ভগ্নাংশ (equivalent fraction), সমহরবিশিষ্ট (same denominator), সমান অংশ (equal parts), অংশ (part).

## Book visuals worth reusing
- Paper folding sequences (square 3 ways, circle 2 ways) — great for animation.
- Roti / bread / cake food models.
- 1-metre ribbon tape with brace and labels.
- Fraction wall (stacked bars 1/2 … 1/10).
- △ for লব, ○ for হর marking.

## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Roti Cutter** — swipe to cut a roti; app checks if parts are equal (area); give halves to Reza & Mina; unfair cuts make one kid sad 😢.
2. **Paper Fold** — tap to fold a square/circle; unfold to see crease lines; shade one part → names appear (½, ¼).
3. **Fraction Wall builder** — drag bars of 1/2, 1/4, 1/8… to match lengths; discover equivalence; "ladder" view.
4. **Ribbon Shop** — cut a 1-m ribbon into n pieces; sell k pieces → k/n m; on a number line ruler.
5. **লব/হর Pizza Party** — order pizza: choose হর (slices) and লব (eaten); see it eaten.
6. **Equivalent Zoom** — split each part again (×2, ×3) with a "knife" — 1/2 → 2/4 → 4/8; numbers multiply live.
7. **Fraction Race** — two kids run 3/8 and 5/8 of a track; who's ahead?
8. **Add/Sub with unit pieces** — fractions as stacks of 1/5 tiles; adding pours tiles together; 1 − 3/8 shows 8/8 bar.
