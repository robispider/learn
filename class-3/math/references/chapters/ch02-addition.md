# অধ্যায় ২ — যোগ (Addition)

- **Book pages:** 21–30 · **PDF pages:** 27–36
- **Range:** 3-digit → 4-digit → 5-digit sums, up to totals of 1,00,000. Adding 2, 3 and 4 numbers.

## Vocabulary
যোগ, যোগফল (sum), হাতে রাখা / হাতে থাকা (carry), উপর-নিচ যোগ (vertical), পাশাপাশি যোগ (horizontal), গাণিতিক বাক্য (number sentence), খালিঘর (blank box).

## Book visuals worth reusing
- **Step-frame strip** (4 grids with arrows and a coloured callout under the active column) — the book's core teaching visual for +, −, ×, ÷. → animate exactly this in our "column machine".
- Coloured column stripes matching place colours.
- Real-life price photos (milk powder, egg tray), cricket, forest.

## Interactive / fun ideas
1. **Column Machine** — numbers drop into a grid; child taps "next" or types the digit per column; the column lights up, a mini base-10 picture shows 9+5=14 → 10 ones squish into a ten-rod that flies over to the next column as the carry "1".
2. **Shop Basket** — pick items with price tags (৳) into a basket, total them; receipt prints.
3. **Cricket Scoreboard** — add runs over 3 days, scoreboard flips digits.
4. **Make 1,00,000** challenge — slider pairs; each column glows green when it makes 10.
5. **Missing-digit detective** — drag digit tiles into ☐ to balance the sum.
6. **Story Maker** — pick characters/objects + numbers → app builds a sentence; child writes/records their own story (teacher-review later).
7. **Card Draw duel** — 6 cards, draw 3, add; vs. Tia the parrot (computer).
